The skill capYour cap goes up on the calendar, not on the mouse

Every account starts with a 700 skill cap and gains 25 more for each month the account exists, up to 1000 at twelve months.

  • 700Your cap on day one
  • 725One month from now
  • 1000Twelve months from now

Stats have their own cap: 250, Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence added together. The stock rule was 225.

You do not have to be here for that to happen.

Three stepsHow to get in

  1. Download the official client

    Free, from uo.com/client-download. It is Broadsword's own signed installer, so your antivirus will not complain. We do not ship a launcher and we never will.

  2. Get Razor and point it here

    Razor is free, from razorce.com. On its first screen there are two fields to fill in:

    Server
    187.127.50.173
    Port
    2593

    Leave the rest alone and press Launch.

    No Razor? Download login.cfg and replace the file of the same name in the folder where Ultima Online is installed. It is a plain text file with two lines; the second one points the game at this server. Then start the game as usual.

  3. Type a name and a password

    At the login screen, type any name and any password. That is your account. It is created at that moment. No registration, no email, no confirmation, nothing to click in your inbox.

    Write your name and password down. There is no email, so for now there is no recovery. If you lose it, it is gone.

First minutesMaking a character

You pick one of three templates, or build your own. A template is where you begin, not a class. Nothing is locked: every skill on the shard is open to every character, and you can leave your starting four behind entirely.

  • WarriorSwordsmanship, Tactics, Anatomy, Healing — 50 each
  • MageMagery, Evaluate Intelligence, Meditation, Inscription — 50 each
  • BlacksmithBlacksmith, Arms Lore, Mining, Tinkering — 50 each
  • ?
    Advancedany skills from the list, 120 points in total

A template gives you 200 skill points, four skills at 50. Advanced lets you pick any skills you want, but only 120 points in total. Both start with 90 stat points.

There is no script for your first session. Nobody meets you at the gate and nobody hands you a quest. You choose what to do, and that is the point.

There is no second, safe copy of the world. There is one Britannia and everyone is in it.

There is custom content, and it is not listed anywhere. Mysteries, hidden mechanics and easter eggs are in the world for whoever finds them.
